NavBuddy Child Safety Standards

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Prevention Policy

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

NavBuddy is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

NavBuddy does not tolerate child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual exploitation of children, grooming, or any other behavior that sexually exploits or endangers minors.

Zero-tolerance policy: Any use of NavBuddy to facilitate, promote, distribute, request, or participate in child sexual abuse or exploitation is strictly prohibited.

2. Prohibited Child-Safety Content and Behavior

Users must not use NavBuddy to create, upload, share, request, distribute, store, or facilitate content or activities involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

The following are strictly prohibited:
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Sexual grooming or manipulation of children.
  • Solicitation of sexual content from minors.
  • Sexualized content involving children.
  • Attempts to arrange or facilitate sexual contact with a minor.
  • Using NavBuddy to identify, target, or exploit children sexually.
  • Trading, requesting, advertising, or distributing CSAM.
  • Any other activity that sexually exploits or endangers a child.

Attempts to circumvent these rules through coded language, links, external services, private communication, or other methods are also prohibited.

3. User Reporting

NavBuddy provides an in-app mechanism that allows users to report inappropriate content and child-safety concerns.

Users are encouraged to report any suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or other behavior that may put a child at risk.

Reports may be reviewed to determine whether they violate NavBuddy's policies. Appropriate action may include removing content, restricting functionality, suspending an account, or permanently terminating an account.

If a child may be in immediate danger, users should contact the appropriate emergency services or law enforcement authority in their location in addition to reporting the concern to NavBuddy.

4. Handling Child-Safety Reports

NavBuddy takes reports involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of children seriously.

When a child-safety report is received, we may take appropriate steps based on the circumstances, including:

  • Reviewing the reported account or content.
  • Taking action against accounts that violate our policies.
  • Preserving relevant information where appropriate and legally permitted.
  • Restricting or removing violating content or accounts.
  • Escalating serious cases to appropriate authorities when required by applicable law.

We may cooperate with law enforcement, government authorities, and relevant child-safety organizations where required or permitted by applicable law.

5. Account Enforcement

NavBuddy may take enforcement action against users who violate this policy.

Depending on the severity and circumstances of the violation, actions may include:

  • Removing violating content.
  • Restricting account functionality.
  • Temporarily suspending an account.
  • Permanently terminating an account.
  • Blocking attempts to create or use accounts for prohibited activity.
  • Reporting serious or legally required cases to appropriate authorities.

NavBuddy may take immediate action when necessary to protect children or other users.

6. Prevention of Child Sexual Exploitation

NavBuddy is designed and operated with user safety in mind. We maintain policies and reporting mechanisms intended to prevent the platform from being used for child sexual abuse or exploitation.

We continuously evaluate reports and safety concerns and may improve our safety procedures as necessary.

Users who encounter content or behavior that may involve the sexual exploitation of a child should report it through the available in-app reporting mechanism or contact our designated child-safety contact.

7. Compliance With Applicable Laws

NavBuddy is committed to complying with applicable child-safety, child-protection, and online-safety laws and regulations relevant to the operation of the service.

Where applicable law requires reporting or cooperation with relevant regional or national authorities concerning suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation, NavBuddy will take appropriate steps to comply with those requirements.
