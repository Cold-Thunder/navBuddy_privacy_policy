Privacy Policy for the NavBuddy mobile application

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Welcome to NavBuddy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information NavBuddy may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you when using the application.

By using NavBuddy, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use a NavBuddy account, we may collect information such as:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number and country code
  • Profile photo
  • Authentication provider information
  • Account creation and update information

Location Information

NavBuddy is designed to help users share their location with selected friends or chat participants. When you enable location sharing, the app may collect and process:

  • Latitude and longitude
  • Location sharing status
  • Location update timestamps

Location information is used to provide location-sharing features and is not intended to be collected when location sharing is disabled.

Messages and Chat Information

If you use NavBuddy's chat functionality, messages and related chat information may be processed to provide messaging functionality.

Technical Information

We may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the application, such as device or application information, error information, and authentication-related information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your NavBuddy account

  • Provide location-sharing functionality
  • Display your profile information to appropriate chat participants
  • Maintain and improve application functionality
  • Detect, prevent, and address security or technical issues
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Location Sharing

NavBuddy may request access to your device's location when you use location-sharing features.

Location sharing is intended to be controlled by you. You can disable location permissions through your device settings and can stop using location-sharing functionality within the application where available.

You should only share your location with people you trust. Please be aware that information shared with another user may be visible to that user according to the application's functionality.
